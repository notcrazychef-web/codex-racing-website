Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy describes the intended Google Drive access for Codex Racing Archive Access and its Intraday Shadow Scanner component, a private research tool operated by its account owner. Cloud Drive intake is not yet deployed.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the application may access selected Google Drive files containing horse-racing selections, race information, archived Telegram messages forwarded into a racing archive, and Betfair market snapshots. It does not request general access to all Google Drive files.

How data is used

Selected data is used for historical research, matching racing selections to market identifiers, checking price movements and evaluating shadow-only predictions. No automatic betting is performed.

Storage and sharing

During development, local exports and research outputs may be stored in private, access-controlled environments operated by the account owner. A future cloud deployment may process the same data using restricted credentials. The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Data is not intentionally shared with third parties except infrastructure providers needed to store or process it under the owner's configuration.

Retention and deletion

Research archives and shadow observations may be retained by the owner for performance analysis. The owner controls deletion of local files, cloud copies and credentials. Retention periods for any future unattended deployment must be documented before activation.

Permissions and revocation

The application uses Google's drive.file scope for specifically authorised files. The owner can revoke Google account access at any time through their Google Account's third-party connections and remove any separately stored credentials.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
