Codex Racing Archive Access

Private research software for the account owner to review UK and Irish horse-racing data, archived selections and historical Betfair Exchange market observations.

The associated Intraday Shadow Scanner is under development. It matches racing selections with market records and evaluates delayed-data observations for research. It does not place bets, offer a public service, or provide executable live odds.

Google Drive access

The application requests the drive.file permission, allowing access only to specific Google Drive files the account owner authorises for the application. Access is used to read racing archives and market-data files. No general access to the owner's Drive is requested.
